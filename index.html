Why I work this way

I came to websites through real estate, but the work was always about people.

I spent years sitting across from people, trying to understand them. Why they do what they do. What they are actually good at. I loved the buildings, but I was always more interested in the person.

People kept telling me they talk to me differently, that things come out they had not planned on saying. It took me a long time to realize that was not just a good conversation. It was the beginning of the work I was meant to build.

Here is what those years taught me. You can put the numbers up. You can say here is the service and here is what it costs. But if I have never met you, how do I know whether to trust you? How do I believe what you say?

That is the part most websites skip, and it is the only part that decides anything.

So I do not start with the site. I start with a conversation about you and the business, and I keep asking until we find the thing you stopped noticing years ago because you have lived with it. Then I build around that.

The best part is not the website. It is watching somebody hear their own story back and realize what they have been sitting on the whole time.

I still buy and sell houses. That side of my work lives at therobertszigeti.com, the tools, the writing, the deals. I built that one too.
