What is a pocket?

Every website I build comes with one of these. A pocket is a digital business card: one page that lives on the owner’s phone, and a person opens it by scanning a code or tapping a link. Paola is a photographer and my founding client, so hers is the one I can show you. A paper card ends up in a drawer. Everyone is on their phone, so that is where hers goes. She has one pocket for couples and one for business owners, and shares the one that fits. Your business should already be in your clients’ pockets when they need you, not something they have to search for.
