Why isn't my business showing up on Google?

Almost always one of six things:

  • An unverified profile
  • The wrong categories
  • Details that don't match
  • Too few reviews
  • A website that’s too vague
  • A competitor doing all of it

Before you fix anything, do this.

Five checks on your phone. Two minutes tells you how big the problem is.

  • Search like a stranger. Phone off wifi. What you do plus the town.
  • Check the phone number. An old number on Google costs you calls.
  • Search Maps too. It ranks separately from Google.
  • Count your reviews. How many, and when was the last one?
  • Open your profile. Not verified? That’s your answer.
What I usually find

The six reasons.

  • Never verified. Creating a profile and verifying it are two steps.
  • Wrong categories. List “contractor” and you go missing for kitchens.
  • Details don’t match. Old numbers and addresses split your trust.
  • Too few reviews. Six against ninety is not a close race.
  • No plain words. Say what you do and the town, in those words.
  • A competitor does it all. Usually someone your size. It’s copyable.
What changed

The part that changed recently.

Google now writes its own answer near the top of the page.

  • It sits under the ads, above the list of businesses.
  • If you’re not in it, many people never scroll far enough to find you.
  • Most advice was written before this, and it’s quietly out of date.
The part nobody warns you about

Frequently asked

Owner questions.

Why does my business show up for me but not for my customers?

Google knows you. You've searched yourself a hundred times. Your customers get none of that, which is why the off-wifi check matters.

My business used to show up. Why did it disappear?

Usually something changed: a move, a new number, or an edit Google paused to review. Check your profile for a notice first.

How long until a new business shows up?

Verification takes days to weeks. Showing up is earned over months, through reviews and a website that says plainly what you do.

Do I need a website to show up on Google Maps?

No. But most people tap your website next, and that's where you win or lose them.

Can I fix this myself for free?

Most of it, yes. It costs your time. Paying someone buys their time and knowing where to look.
