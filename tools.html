The ones I hear most

What falls through the cracks when the week gets busy?

None of these are a website problem. All of them cost an owner the same thing, which is the part of the week they never get back.

01

The estimate you sent that went quiet.

You priced it, you sent it, and then nothing. Chasing it feels like begging, so it sits there. Most of those were never a no. They were just never followed up.

02

The call that came in while you were under a sink.

By the time you wipe your hands and call back, they have already booked the next guy. The work was yours. The phone just rang at the wrong minute.

03

The question your office asks you three times a week.

Always the same question, always to you, because the answer has never lived anywhere except in your head. It is not that nobody can learn it. Nobody can look it up.

04

The job you will not know made money until it is over.

Labor, materials, the change nobody wrote down, the extra day. You find out whether it was worth doing weeks after you could have done anything about it.
