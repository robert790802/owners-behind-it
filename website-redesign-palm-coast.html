A fresh start shouldn’t throw away what you’ve built.

Your domain, content and customer relationships matter. We discuss what stays, what changes and what needs moving before replacing the old site.

  • Your domain and ownership

    The site is built for your business to own. Keeping your existing domain is usually possible; we check access and hosting arrangements first.

    We identify content worth keeping and plan where changed addresses should lead, so a redesign does not casually discard years of work.

    The pages explain what you do and where you work, with the behind-the-scenes information search engines need to read them. We also keep the counters that show visits and actions. No redesign comes with a guaranteed Google position.

Before you replace your website.

Can I keep my existing domain?

Usually, yes. We check who controls the domain, where the site is hosted and what needs to move. A redesign does not automatically require a new web address.

Will a redesign affect my Google rankings?

It can. Changing content and URLs can change how search engines understand the site. We review the existing pages, plan redirects where needed and check the search foundations at launch. Rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Can we improve part of the site instead of rebuilding everything?

We can start by looking at that. Whether a focused update makes sense depends on the platform, access, existing structure and the problem you want to solve. The first conversation helps us decide whether I can help. A detailed review can be scoped as paid work.

How long does a website redesign take?

The schedule depends on the number of pages, the content available, feedback and any migration or custom functionality. I give you a project-specific schedule with the scope, rather than promising the same turnaround for every business.

Do I need to write all the content myself?

The work begins with a conversation about your business. Your experience, services and real examples give us the material to shape the story together. We agree on content responsibilities as part of the scope.

Do you work with businesses outside Palm Coast?

Yes. I work with owners around Flagler County, St. Augustine and North Florida, and can meet by video when that is more practical. Tell me where you are and what you need when we talk.
