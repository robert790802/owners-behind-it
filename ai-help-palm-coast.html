    How can AI help my business?

    On the work you repeat every week: every inquiry caught in one place and sorted by what’s urgent, follow-up that goes out on its own after the call, the quote and the finished job, review requests when the customer is happiest, and online booking with reminders.

    How can AI help with my job estimates?

    It starts before the estimate. Your site asks a few useful questions up front, like what they need, details and photos, so you’re not driving out for a free look. You send a clean estimate from your phone, and when an estimate sits, a reminder goes out on its own, so the customer isn’t the one chasing.

    How can I get back to my customers faster using AI?

    Every inquiry lands in one place, sorted by what’s urgent, instead of scattered across voicemail, texts and email. The customer gets an automatic reply right away saying you got it and when you’ll reach out, so they know they weren’t ignored, even when you’re on a job.

    How do I use AI to automate booking for my clients?

    Your clients book online, right from your site, into times that come straight from your own calendar. They get a confirmation right away and a reminder before the appointment, so fewer people forget and you’re not playing phone tag to set a time.

    Can AI automate my invoices?

    Yes. I already use AI for the invoices in my own cleaning business. For yours, I build it around how you already bill: when a job is marked finished, the invoice goes out on its own, and if it sits unpaid, a reminder follows. You stop chasing payments at the end of the day.

    Can AI answer customer questions on my website?

    Yes. Your site can have a helper that answers customers’ questions any time, from your own approved answers only.     Can my business actually make more money using AI?

    Yes. AI can definitely help you find where you’re losing money. When every call, quote and invoice lands in one place, you can see what slipped: the call nobody returned, the quote that sat, the invoice still unpaid. Then it helps you stop it. The sit-down is where we find yours.

    No, and you wouldn’t want it to. Your customers chose you. What AI can do is carry the repeat work around you, so the calls, quotes and follow-ups stop waiting on you and you’re free for the part only you can do.
